# labs/qec03 · Quality Engineering Core · chapter 3 · Defect management and root cause analysis

Practice files for the chapter 3 demo and Task HLF-P53 (*Bundle & Save defects and a root cause*, about 70 min). **Not scored. Unlimited attempts.** Everything here is fictional.

> **The bookshop** is the practice online bookshop that Priya's Tasks use. It is separate from Harbourleaf, the store in the videos.

## You need
- A spreadsheet: Google Sheets, Excel or LibreOffice Calc. Open the `.csv` files in it.
- **diagrams.net** in your browser (free, no account): https://app.diagrams.net. For the fishbone: Arrange, Insert, Template, **Business**, then an Ishikawa template. Choose to save in your browser or on your device.
- A notes file for your answers.

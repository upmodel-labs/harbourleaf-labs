# Lab qec05 · HLF-P55 · Lead the Bundle & Save second run

Part of UpModel, module Quality Engineering Core, chapter 5 (Leading Quality Work: Team Leadership & Mentoring).

**Practice data only.** The practice online bookshop is separate from Harbourleaf. Every person, order, defect and number in these files is made up for teaching. Never paste real or work data into an AI assistant or anywhere else.

## What you need

- A spreadsheet: Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice (all free to use in a browser or on a computer).
- A notes file: any text editor, a Google Doc or a notes app.
- Nothing else. You do not run any code in this lab.
- For the last step: any free AI assistant.

## The task in one paragraph

Priya, the QA (quality assurance) Lead, is on the Room Match launch calls all week. You lead the testing of the practice bookshop's Bundle & Save second run. A new tester joins today. You plan the week, share out the work, help the new tester, keep the bookshop's product owner informed and agree with the developer how fixes come back. The Task has 8 steps and takes 70 minutes.

## How to work

1. Do steps 1 to 7 yourself, in a spreadsheet and a notes file. Give a reason for every choice, and write everything you would send.
2. Do step 8 last. Do the manual work first, then ask an AI assistant to draft feedback on the same two practice defect reports, check it, and decide what you keep. The assistant only drafts. You decide and you send.
3. Check your own work with the "Good looks like" line of each step and the rules in `task/status_note_template.md`. Your answer can differ from someone else's where the rule allows: any good answer must pass the checks in the step.

# Bundle & Save · feature note (Task HLF-P51)

Practice material for the module Quality Engineering Core, chapter 1. The practice online bookshop is fictional and separate from Harbourleaf.

**The offer:** add any 3 paperbacks to the cart and the cheapest one is free. With 6 paperbacks, 2 are free: the cheapest in each group of 3, with the groups formed from the highest price down.
- Hardbacks and ebooks do not count towards a group of 3.
- The cart shows a "Bundle saving" line, plus a one-line message written by a model from the cart's titles, formats, prices and descriptions, such as "Add one more paperback to save 12.99".
- Free shipping starts at 25.00 on the total after the saving.
- The offer runs through 23:59:59 store time on 30 November. The clock is the store's, not the customer's.

**Three services:** the catalogue (prices and formats), the cart (applies the offer and writes the message) and checkout (the total).
- Prices can change in the catalogue during the campaign.

**The team:** 4 testers, sharing one test site with the web team. The campaign starts in 3 weeks, and the final catalogue prices arrive in week 2.

Use the offer rules and the team facts as input when you try the AI step. Practice files only, never real or work data.

# Room Match · feature note (demo)

Practice material for the module Quality Engineering Core, chapter 1. Harbourleaf and Room Match are fictional.

**What it does:** a customer uploads a photo of a room, or describes it in words, and gets product suggestions (rugs, lamps, cushions).

**Three parts**
- The photo reader: turns a photo into a short description of the room.
- The suggestions service: finds products; shows the size, the stock (from the stock feed) and the price in rupees or Canadian dollars.
- A model that writes a one-line "why it fits" for each suggestion.

Customers can also save their suggestions as a PDF to share.

**Launch:** in five weeks, in India and Canada.

**The team:** a small team. The sample room photos come from the Canada team.
